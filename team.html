Buffer & risk
The schedule has no formal buffer week. Phase 5 (hardening) absorbs minor overruns. If a Phase 2 or 3 deliverable slips by more than 3 days, the team must cut scope from Phase 4 secondary features (search, analytics, social share) — not push the launch date.
